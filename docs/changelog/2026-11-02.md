## 2026-11-02

- Automatic shipping prices ignore preorder products. A shipment made only of preorder products is still offered, at price 0, and records that the shipping amount is still to be agreed.
- An order that still needs a preorder phone consultation shows that notice and the customer's phone on the order page. Until staff mark the consultation done, the order does not generate an AWB, an invoice, or an ERP send. A normal order is unchanged.
- Update Status on an order that still needs a phone consultation asks staff to confirm before the status form opens. Cancel leaves the order unchanged.
- Consultation done opens a modal. Staff choose the payment method and, unless delivery is store collection, the full shipping price, starting from the weight-based price for every product. Card moves the order to Awaiting Payment, bank transfer to Awaiting Bank Transfer, and cash on delivery to Payment Offline. The agreed shipping total and the consultation are stored before that status change. The action does not create an AWB or send the order to the ERP.
