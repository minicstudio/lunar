## 2026-10-13

- Buy X Get Y condition quantity now counts packs when `unit_quantity` > 1 (`ProductVariant::giftConditionQuantity()`), so “Buy 1 Get 1” grants one gift per package. Gift budget trimming prefers dropping non-selected leftover gift lines first.
- When cart lines carry `selected_gift_rewards` (including explicit `[]` skips), gift line budget is capped to selection demand so deleting a gifted line cannot leave its gift on a skipped sibling.
