## 2026-11-20

- Staff can duplicate an order from the order screen. They choose a payment method and, when the lines ship, a shipping method and a price that starts as that method's calculated price. The new order is created already in the status configured for the payment method, so a card payment sends the payment-link email and a bank transfer sends its instructions, as a split does. Duplication takes stock for the copied lines. A later shipment status does not take that stock again. It does not dispatch `OrderPlacedEvent`.
