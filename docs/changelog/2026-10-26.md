## 2026-10-26

- Staff can change product lines on an unpaid order that has not been sent to the ERP, invoiced, or given an AWB.
- Adding a product starts with the product. When it has more than one variant, staff choose one of those variants. A product with a single variant is selected automatically.
- Saving a product line recalculates the order totals. A rejected save leaves the order unchanged.
- Stock moves only by the line difference when a stock-decreasing status is still the latest stock operation.
