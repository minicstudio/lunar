## 2026-11-24

- Product import: the AI now gives every list, category, and packed-items column its own list, path, and item separators (`none` for one item or one level), and each sheet its weight and length units chosen from the store's unit codes (LFP-954). Separators are no longer import options and have no defaults, so two sheets of one import can separate their categories differently; staff correct them per column in the mapping step, and profiles keep them. The column profile no longer guesses a column type: every column sends up to 30 samples (`product-import.ai.sample_size`). Meta title, description, and keywords columns map to the product type's attributes, labelled with their attribute group ("Search Engine Optimization › Title"), and a URL slug column (`product.slug`) gives new products their URL in that language, with a number suffix when another product uses it; existing products keep their URLs. Images that failed to download are listed on the result page with their reason, staff can correct their URLs, and **Retry failed images** downloads them again without writing any product. The row correction form now shows corrected prices, numbers, dates, codes, and lists the way their column reads them.
