## 2026-11-26

- LFP-924: Added recording of add-to-cart, cart start and checkout start events (`lunar_conversion_events`, no personal data) and a Conversions report in the admin Analytics group (`analytics/conversions`): adds to cart, carts, checkouts and purchases for a period, the checkout, purchase and cart purchase rates, the four steps as a funnel chart and per day, week or month, both charts with only the purchases from tracked carts. Purchases count every counted order and show how many come from tracked carts (carts with recorded events); only those divide the purchase rates, as on the Traffic sources report. Recording is off until `ANALYTICS_CONVERSIONS_ENABLED=true` (independent of traffic recording) and needs the storefront to call `ConversionEventRecorder::addToCart()` and `::beginCheckout()`; the report needs the new `analytics:conversions` permission, which `php artisan migrate` creates. Purchases count as on the Traffic sources report, whose period filter, grouping and order rules are now shared without changing its figures.
- Traffic visits now count only once a browser confirms them: the first page view of a session stores the visit unconfirmed, and a second page view, an add to cart or a presence script on the landing page, which the host includes in its layout, confirms it through the new `POST /lunar/presence` route. Crawlers with browser user agents that keep no cookies and run no scripts, which inflated Direct traffic, are left out of the Traffic sources report; their unconfirmed visits are pruned after 30 days. Visits stored before keep counting. Adds `lunar_traffic_visits.confirmed_at`.
- LFP-924: Carts now get their traffic source when a product is first added to them instead of when they are created. The empty cart created after a completed order no longer counts as a cart in the Traffic sources report, and for logged-in customers it no longer holds back new visits or passes the previous visit's source to their next order. Carts stored before the change keep their source.
