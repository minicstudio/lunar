## 2026-11-26

- LFP-924: Added recording of add-to-cart, cart start and checkout start events (`lunar_conversion_events`, no personal data) and a Conversions report in the admin Analytics group (`analytics/conversions`): adds to cart, carts, checkouts and purchases for a period, the checkout, purchase and cart purchase rates, and the four steps per day, week or month. Purchases count every counted order and show how many come from tracked carts (carts with recorded events); only those divide the purchase rates, as on the Traffic sources report. Recording is off until `ANALYTICS_CONVERSIONS_ENABLED=true` (independent of traffic recording) and needs the storefront to call `ConversionEventRecorder::addToCart()` and `::beginCheckout()`; the report needs the new `analytics:conversions` permission, which `php artisan migrate` creates. Purchases count as on the Traffic sources report, whose period filter, grouping and order rules are now shared without changing its figures.
