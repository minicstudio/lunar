## 2026-11-03

- Added a Free shipping option to Advanced amount off discounts (LFP-863). With a coupon code, shipping is free when the coupon qualifies; without one, when any cart product is eligible for the discount, whichever discount prices that product. The discount may also take nothing off products. Every shipping option is charged at 0, the cart reports the free-shipping discount and the waived amount, and the order shipping line keeps the option price with the waived amount as its discount. A shipping-only discount is listed in the order's discount breakdown, so it counts as used and a shipping-only coupon is the order's applied coupon. Admin product-line edits keep or remove free shipping as the discount still qualifies or not.
- Changed: an automatic discount that takes nothing off products no longer hides a lower-tier discount in the cart or the catalog. Carts with an explicit shipping override, such as order split targets, keep the entered shipping price. Free shipping is waived by the new cart stage `ApplyDiscountFreeShipping`, which hosts must add to their published `config/lunar/cart.php` directly before `CalculateTax`.
