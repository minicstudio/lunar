## 2026-10-12

- Cart `unitPriceWithoutCoupon` / `unitPriceWithoutCouponIncTax` now use the per-quantity amount (catalog price ÷ `unit_quantity`) instead of the raw pack price, so storefront “Qty × price” matches the line subtotal. `AdvancedAmountOff` line discounts use the same per-quantity base.
- Admin inventory: `quantity_increment` must be a multiple of `unit_quantity` (validated on save; changing unit quantity snaps an invalid increment to the new unit quantity).
