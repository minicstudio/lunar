## 2026-10-26

- DomPDF invoice download availability uses status eligibility (`isLunarInvoiceEligible`), not `lunar_invoice_generated_at`; that stamp remains email-dispatch idempotency only. Renamed `dispatchInvoiceGeneratedUnlessEmailed`.
- Invoice billing is provider-agnostic: `InvoiceDocumentService` / observers no longer hardcode SmartBill; complimentary line pricing lives on `OrderLine::isComplimentary()` / `invoiceUnitPrice()`.

## 2026-10-22

- Lunar DomPDF bank-transfer invoice trigger default is now `awaiting-bank-transfer` (`config/lunar/invoicing.php`).
- `LunarInvoiceObserver` always invoices on `completed` in addition to the per-payment-option configured status.

## 2026-10-21

- SmartBill PDF download no longer requires `actions.billing`: historical invoices remain downloadable when billing generation is switched to Lunar DomPDF (`ErpService::downloadInvoicePDF`, Filament download action fallback).
- Invoice document API: `InvoiceDocumentService`, `OrderInvoiceGenerated`, Lunar DomPDF observer (`LunarInvoiceObserver`) with `config/lunar/invoicing.php` payment-option status map; SmartBill `OrderObserver` dispatches the email event after successful generation without changing `generate_invoice` statuses.
- SmartBill payload: shipping uses `measuring_unit_name` and store tax-inclusion; complimentary/BXGY lines at price 0; coupon tax aligned with product lines when uniform; `PaymentSlugMapper` covers `bank-transfer` and `stripe-wallet-pay`.
