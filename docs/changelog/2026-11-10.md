## 2026-11-10

- Pack-priced lines (`unit_quantity` > 1) are now clear on invoices (LFP-935). SmartBill invoices them in packs: unit `pachet` (`SMARTBILL_PACK_MEASURING_UNIT_NAME`), quantity = packs, price = pack price, and a "1 pachet = 4 buc" product description. Lines sold per piece are unchanged, so one invoice can mix `buc` and `pachet` lines. Set `SMARTBILL_INVOICE_PACKS=false` to keep pack lines in pieces with only the description. The pack unit must exist in the SmartBill account and be mapped to an e-Factura code (e.g. XPK). A line whose quantity is not a whole number of packs stays in pieces.
- Fixed SmartBill invoices on stores with prices stored excluding tax: shipping and the coupon line were sent with VAT included while flagged as excluding VAT, so SmartBill added VAT again (e.g. shipping 15 + 21% sent as 18,15 net, a 39 lei coupon sent as -47,19), and the invoice total was lower than the order total. Both are now sent on the same basis as the product lines (`shipping_sub_total`, `coupon_total_without_tax`).
- Redeemed loyalty points are now on the SmartBill invoice and the Lunar PDF as a negative "Reducere puncte de fidelitate" line (code `LOIALITATE`, amount from `meta.loyalty_discount_minor`, with the same VAT rate as the coupon line). The product lines are sent before the loyalty reduction, so without this line the invoice total was higher than the amount paid (e.g. 504,00 instead of 502,00 for 200 points).
- Pack prices are derived from the unrounded line amount, so packs × price matches the order total. Before, the truncated per-piece price could lose a few bani per line (e.g. 99,99 → 4 × 24,99 = 99,96).
- The Lunar DomPDF invoice / admin order PDF has a U.M. column: pack lines are listed as `pachet` with the pack contents under the product name. Before, it showed the pack price next to the piece quantity (4 × 100 lei for a 100 lei line).
- The same PDF now lists the lines like the SmartBill invoice: `#`, name, U.M., quantity, unit price excluding VAT, value and VAT per line; products at their price before the coupon, then "Cost de livrare" and a negative "Cupon de reducere - CODE" line; the footer has the totals excluding VAT and of VAT, then "Total factură". The VAT rate is in the header, the currency once under each amount column header, and the amounts are right-aligned without it. Amounts are excluding VAT whether prices are stored inclusive of tax or not. Before, with `LUNAR_STORE_INCLUSIVE_OF_TAX=true` the unit price, discount and subtotal were printed with VAT included, and the footer did not add up when a discount applied. Orders without a tax rate above 0% (seller not registered for VAT) show only the unit price and value, and "Total factură".
- SmartBill invoices of orders without a tax rate above 0% (seller not registered for VAT) are sent without VAT fields (`isTaxIncluded`, `taxName`, `taxPercentage`), as SmartBill expects for a company that is not a VAT payer. Before, such an order failed when `tax_names` had no `0` entry or no default tax zone existed.
- `OrderLine` (and its contract) gains `packSize()`, `isInvoicedInPacks()`, `invoicePackQuantity()` and `invoicePackPrice()`. `SmartbillProduct` accepts an optional `productDescription`, and its `taxName` and `taxPercentage` may be null. The PDF lines come from `Lunar\Admin\Support\Orders\OrderInvoicePdf`, handed to `lunarpanel::pdf.order` as `$invoice` by a view composer, so callers and published overrides of the view get it without changes.
