## 2026-11-23

- Staff can watch storefront session replays from Analytics. The screen is available when `packstub/session-replay` is installed. The permission is `settings:view-session-replays`; staff marked as admin can open it without that permission being assigned. The package's own viewer stays off. Playback uses the `viewSessionReplay` gate for that same permission, on the staff guard, with the web middleware.
- Session replay customer column shows the storefront email when the recording is linked to a user, Anonymous when the shopper was signed in without statistics cookies, and Guest otherwise.
